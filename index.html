// 01 · iOS engineering · 3D design and shading

I love developing iOS and Mac apps that bring photos into 3D.

I've built iOS apps for years, and 3D has been my thing since school: half a year in Cinema 4D at a media agency, then computer graphics at university. 3Dify brings both together. It estimates depth from a single photo with on-device CoreML models and renders it with Metal shaders, much like this portrait turning into a 3D point cloud. // 02 · Cloud infrastructure engineering

At SAP, I helped build the next generation cloud for Europe.

I worked on CobaltCore, the new KVM stack behind SAP's global cloud of 20+ datacenters and 100,000+ virtual machines, and the reference implementation of ApeiroRA, the Apeiro Reference Architecture for Europe under the governance of Linux Foundation Europe. My focus was scheduling and inventory: deciding where every workload runs.

// 03 · Go · Kubernetes · OpenStack

I am the lead contributor of Cortex, a smart cloud workload scheduler.

I built it from the ground up for SAP Cloud Infrastructure: it places every new KVM virtual machine and continuously rebalances the inventory based on real utilization data, bridging OpenStack scheduling and a cloud-native Kubernetes world. I also helped develop central Kubernetes components, like the VM operator and the KVM node agent, that drive thousands of customer-critical workloads today.

// 04 · Agentic development and operations

I built agentic systems that developers use today to move faster.

Around CobaltCore, AI agents take routine work off engineers and operators. An agentic review and release pipeline checks pull requests and prepares releases, fully integrated into the project's workflow, while a knowledge graph links issues to the commits that resolved them. The agents get their inference from Thalamus and reach the network only through an Envoy proxy that sandboxes them. My prototype for predicting host outages with SAP-RPT-1 won 2nd place at an international SAP hackathon.

// 06 · TU Dresden · PhD research

I know how to build strong data-driven solutions, like machine learning models.

For my PhD at TU Dresden (Dr.-Ing., 2024), I developed machine-learning models for smart-city use cases and evaluated them on real-world data: matching traffic lights and lanes to bike routes, measuring how predictable 18,009 traffic lights in Hamburg are, and detecting transport modes on phones.

// 07 · Lead engineer · Flutter · Go · Python

I built PrioBike, a green wave app for cyclists.

As lead engineer and PhD student, I took PrioBike from idea to field test in Hamburg. The app gives cyclists speed advice to catch the green wave, based on live data from 10,000+ traffic lights. I wrote the probabilistic traffic-light prediction in Go, the ML matching of traffic lights to routes in Python, and a GraphHopper-based bike routing more accurate than Google Maps and OSM. PrioBike won 3rd place at the Deutscher Fahrradpreis.
