// 02 · Cloud infrastructure · KVM

At SAP, I help build the next-generation sovereign cloud for Europe.

CobaltCore is the new KVM stack behind SAP's cloud: 20+ datacenters running 100,000+ virtual machines. It is also the reference implementation of ApeiroRA, the Apeiro Reference Architecture for Europe, governed by Linux Foundation Europe. I help develop its central Kubernetes components, like the VM operator and the KVM node agent, and my focus is scheduling: deciding where every workload runs.

// 03 · Scheduling · Go · Kubernetes

I'm the lead contributor of Cortex, the scheduler that decides where SAP's VMs run.

I built Cortex from the ground up for SAP Cloud Infrastructure. It places every new KVM virtual machine and continuously rebalances the fleet based on real utilization data, bridging OpenStack scheduling and the cloud-native Kubernetes world.

// 04 · Agentic engineering

I build AI agents that take routine work off our engineers and operators.

Around CobaltCore, my agents review pull requests and prepare releases, fully integrated into the project's workflow, while a knowledge graph links issues to the commits that resolved them. To keep this safe, the agents get their inference from Thalamus and reach the network only through an Envoy proxy that sandboxes them.

// 06 · PhD research · TU Dresden

For my PhD, I put machine learning on real streets.

At TU Dresden (Dr.-Ing., 2024), I developed machine learning models for smart cities and evaluated them on real-world data. How predictable are traffic lights? How do you match them, lane by lane, to a bike route? Which models detect your transport mode on a phone without wasting its resources? Most of the answers went straight into PrioBike.

  • Cloudy with a Chance of Green: Measuring the Predictability of 18,009 Traffic Lights in HamburgIEEE IV 2024
  • GeoAI-Powered Lane Matching for Bike Routes in GLOSA AppsACM SIGSPATIAL 2023
  • Matching Traffic Lights to Routes for Real-World Deployments of Mobile GLOSA AppsIEEE ISC2 2022
  • Selecting Resource-Efficient ML Models for Transport Mode Detection on Mobile DevicesIEEE IoTaIS 2022

// 07 · PrioBike · Flutter · Go · Python

I built PrioBike, a green wave app for cyclists.

As lead engineer and PhD student, I took PrioBike from idea to field test in Hamburg. The app gives cyclists speed advice to catch the green wave, based on live data from 18,000+ traffic lights. I wrote the probabilistic traffic-light prediction in Go, brought the matching from my research into production in Python, and built a GraphHopper-based bike routing more accurate than Google Maps. PrioBike won 3rd place at the Deutscher Fahrradpreis.
